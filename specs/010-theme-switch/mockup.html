What this mockup covers

  • Placement of the theme control next to the sign-out button, icon-only, for signed-in visitors (FR-002).
  • Same control, equivalent top-right position, for signed-out visitors with no sign-out button (FR-003).
  • Clicking the toggle in the canvas actually re-themes the shell live, standing in for the real light/dark switch (FR-004, FR-009).
  • Desktop vs. mobile layout, since the sidebar collapses to a top bar on mobile (existing shell behavior).

Out of scope for this mockup (see spec.md / design.md): persistence across visits (FR-005), OS-preference default detection (FR-007), and screen-reader announcement wiring (FR-008) — these are behavioral/data concerns with no distinct visual state beyond what the toggle already shows.
