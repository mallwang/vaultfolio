Out of scope for this mockup

  • Real PDF rendering: the sheet approximates the rebuilt page (static text at original positions). Fonts and exact positions come from the shared renderer.
  • German texts: the mockup is English only; every string exists in English and German in the implementation (FR-045).
  • Validation and storage rules (strict schema, server-side personal-data scan, retention sweep, request limits) are behaviour, not layout.
  • Words of the sheet are invented. Marked words are three-way: pending, kept, masked.

Visual language

Approximates the PrimeNG Aura preset, using the same tokens as the Earnings mockup (032). Exact tokens come from the app theme at implementation time.
