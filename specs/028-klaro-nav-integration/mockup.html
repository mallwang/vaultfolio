Klaro

Contract & subscription management

Klaro helps you keep track of every contract and subscription you're paying for — insurance policies, housing, utilities, streaming and software subscriptions, and anything else that recurs. It gives you a dashboard of your total monthly spend broken down by category, and warns you ahead of time when a contract is coming up for renewal, so nothing auto-renews as a surprise.

  • Monthly spend & category breakdown
  • Renewal alerts before contracts auto-renew
  • A sortable table of every contract you've recorded
  • Export & import your data (JSON / Excel)

Klaro is currently its own, standalone application.

It isn't built into Vaultfolio yet — you use it by opening it separately, with its own sign-in.

Open Klaro

Opens klaro.allwang.family in a new tab — your Vaultfolio session stays open here.

Where this is headed

  • Today

    Create your own account directly in Klaro to start using it — Vaultfolio sign-in doesn't carry over yet.

  • Sync

    Once available, your Klaro data can be synchronized with Vaultfolio — but only if you use the identical email address for both your Vaultfolio and Klaro accounts (markus@example.com in both, for instance).

  • Future

    Klaro's full functionality will eventually move into Vaultfolio itself, right here under this same "Klaro" navigation entry.

This is a static, non-functional mockup for design review — no data is saved, no backend is called, and the external link is inert here.

What this covers

  • US1 (P1): the "Klaro" nav entry with its own logo mark, positioned like the other domain entries (FR-001, FR-002).
  • US2 (P1): the info page's description of Klaro, the standalone-app notice, and the external link opening in a new tab (FR-003–FR-006).
  • US3 (P2): the "Where this is headed" card — separate account today, future sync requiring identical email addresses, future full integration (FR-007–FR-009).

Not shown here (out of scope for this mockup)
